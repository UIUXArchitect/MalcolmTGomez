About Me.txt
Malcolm T. Gomez / DFW

Strategy-minded. Story-driven.

I work across sports, entertainment, partnerships and innovation. I like messy business questions, ambitious live experiences, emerging technology and the work of turning all of that into a clearer story and a stronger strategy.

I came into this chapter with a background in talent and recruiting, then moved deeper into sports business, live events, technology, and sponsorship. I tend to look at a problem from both sides: what makes the business work, and what makes people care.
